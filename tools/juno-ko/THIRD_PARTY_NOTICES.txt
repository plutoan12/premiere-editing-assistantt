The pop animation, layered shadow and active-word highlighting are adapted
from Juno. This preview adds Korean-first typography, measured wrapping,
explicit overflow handling and a text-only PEA test-draft exporter.
No tscaps Studio/AGPL application code, font files, or MOGRT assets are included.
This is not a tscaps engine integration or a faithful MOGRT conversion.
